# Liminal House

Welcome to the liminal house, a vanilla themed interior meant to act as a counterpart to the Manor! Full of cozy rooms, beautiful fake windows and plenty of interactable doodads, you'll want to stay forever. There's even tons of great paintings!

Unfortunately, this house already has inhabitants...
