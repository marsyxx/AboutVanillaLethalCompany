# Utility Drone

A new ship upgrade for the operator role, providing a fully customizable drone! The drone supports interchangeable modules as well as voice relay, loot cargo, and custom paint schemes. **All players need the mod!**

## Getting started

Buy the **Utility Drone station** from the terminal with `utilitydronestation` (or `utility-drone-station`). Hold E on the charging station's screen and press **PILOT DRONE** to take the pilot tablet, then left click with it once the ship lands. Only whoever carries the tablet can fly the drone (also with `utilitydrone` at the terminal). Drop it and it goes back to the station.

## Modules

Drones start empty. Fit up to **four modules** (the host can allow 0 to 5) at the charging station:

- **Zap gun:** Stun enemies with the drone's zap gun.
- **Defibrillator:** Revive a dead teammate (customizable number of uses per moon and battery cost)
- **Lockpicker:** Unlock locked facility doors.
- **Spray paint:** Unlimited green spray paint.
- **Weed killer:** Unlimited weed killer.
- **Healing beam:** Slowly heal an injured teammate.
- **Trap hacker:** Blow up landmines, turrets and spike traps, plus the turrets from Devious Traps. Pings every 5 seconds to mark traps within 20 m.
- **Magnet:** Pick up and carry teammates, their bodies, or the cruiser.
- **Boombox:** Play music! Plays your CustomBoomboxMusic songs too, if you have that mod.
- **Stim injector:** Shoot a teammate with a stimulant that makes them stronger and faster for a short time.
- **Self destruct:** A huge bomb that wipes out everything nearby. Once per mission.
- **Shotgun:** Fires shotgun shells from the drone's cargo.
- **Battery charger:** Allows teammates to recharge their equipment using the drone's underside.
- **Thermal scanner:** See enemies through walls within 25 meters while piloting.
- **Stun launcher:** Fire stun grenades. Holds 3, refilled at the charging dock.
- **Beacon:** Lights everything within 35 meters, and your crew can see the drone through walls.
- **Pinger:** Mark up to three spots with your own labels: one red, one blue and one green. Your whole crew sees them through walls until the mission ends.
- **Grapple winch:** Hook an item, a crewmate or a dead crewmate's body up to 15 meters away and reel them in to the drone, or yank open cabinet and locker doors and knock snare fleas off ceilings and heads.

Some modules share a mount, so only one of them can be fitted at a time. Supports a max of four modules at once by default; the host can set Modules / MaxModules from 0 to 5.

## Customization

Hold **E** on the station to open management:

- **Workshop:** Choose modules and a battery. Batteries trade capacity, speed and charging rate.
- **Paint shop:** Change the body and chassis colors.
- **Presets:** Ready-made drones to get an idea of how to build your drone.
- **Recall Drone:** Bring the drone home without typing a command.

Choices save with the ship and sync for everyone.

## Other features

- **Cargo:** Carry items in the drone. Heavier loads fly slower.
- **Support:** Item scanning, a headlight, employee tracking through walls and voice relay.
- **Enemy interactions!** Some enemies interact with the drone. Attacking it, grabbing it, or messing with it in various ways. It can interact with enemies in turn, such as staring at the coilhead.
- **Automatic return:** Flies home with its cargo, even if it runs out of battery or gets heavily damaged!

## Controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / Mouse | Fly / Look |
| Space / Left Ctrl | Up / Down |
| Left Shift | Boost |
| E | Use doors and facility entrances |
| Tap E on drone | Open cargo |
| Hold E on station | Open management |
| Mouse wheel | Switch modules |
| LMB | Use module |
| RMB | Scan |
| F | Headlight |
| Hold G | Recall the drone to its charging station |
| X | Show or hide the ship's radar monitor |
| C | Switch the radar monitor to the next crewmate (while it is shown) |
| T | Switch between hearing the drone's surroundings and your own body on the ship |
| M | Free a drone stuck in a door or ceiling (short cooldown) |
| Esc | Exit drone view |

Keybinds, mouse sensitivity, the management window's size, the station's price, the drone's max cargo weight (5 to 50 lb), which modules are available, the defibrillator's revives per moon and its battery cost, and whether the pilot tablet works outside the ship are configurable.
