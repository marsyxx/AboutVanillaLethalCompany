# Changelog

## 1.3.0
- Idle-audio sweep every 30 s (`SweepSeconds`, `IdleMinutes`), not just in orbit.
- Memory reports are now off by default.

## 1.2.0
- Texture mipmap limit (`MipmapLimit`, default 1 = half resolution).

## 1.1.0
- Memory reports at the menu and after landing.

## 1.0.0
- Frees idle Decompress-On-Load audio and unloads unused assets when the ship reaches orbit.
