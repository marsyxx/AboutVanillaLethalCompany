- Blacksite soldiers can be hunted and devoured by a modded predator that registers with Contracted, such as Y4NGZMonsters' Crooked Choir. ## 1.0.1

- Blacksite: on clients, soldier presentation (animator parameters, aim pose, helmet light, equipment visuals) now runs at a reduced rate for soldiers that are far from, or not visible to, the local camera, with hysteresis at the tier edges; a distant soldier still in view, or out of view within 40 m (so his footsteps keep their rhythm through a wall), keeps animating at a slow rate instead of freezing, a baton soldier's hit flinch and baton cues still play on time, and the pose snaps only when a soldier comes back from the far tier; on the host only the helmet light follows the tier, because its combat logic reads the aim pose and animator state. Host AI, footstep audio and everything replicated are unchanged, and the rifle-visibility pass no longer allocates every frame. `Blacksite Soldier Presentation LOD` (Diagnostics, default true) turns it off for a local A/B (#1433).
- Blacksite soldier deployment and other contract runtime work (the Blacksite planner tick, the Wesley side-objective poll) now wait for a frame with spare time instead of stacking into one long frame (#1433).
- Blacksite soldiers, the Congregation, the Seraphim and the Whistleblower keep seeing Y4NGZDebugTools dummies while the host's mob ignore is on, and the Stranger's Bundy no longer chooses the ignored player as a new target; requires Y4NGZCore 1.0.11 (Y4NGZPerformance#48).
- Round-end performance report: sprites are prepared during landing and the report is assembled over a few frames, removing a ~110 ms hitch (#1433).
- Diagnostic logging during normal play is now off unless `Performance Timing Logging` is on: dungeon generation heartbeats, Seraphim frame costs, round-end report timings under 50 ms, and special-interaction input stack dumps (these still print after a stuck-input warning); the payload cart body hierarchy dump now needs `Shadow Raid Debug Logging` (#1405, #1433).
